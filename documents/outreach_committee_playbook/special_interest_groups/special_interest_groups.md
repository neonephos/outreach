# Working Topics and Special Interest Groups

In addition to the [General Assembly meeting](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/general_assembly/minutes), the Outreach Committee hosts permanent, focused get-togethers called Special Interest Groups (SIGs).
SIGs of the Outreach Committee are permanent working groups that focus on a particular topic of interest.

The current SIGs of the Outreach Committee are:

- [Events](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/events)
- [Social Media & Marketing](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/social_media)
- [Ambassador](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/ambassador_program)
- [KPI](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/kpi)

## Creation of a SIG

- SIGs are created in the Outreach Committee through simple majority vote.

## Membership

As decided in [#50](https://github.com/orgs/neonephos/projects/20/views/1?pane=issue&itemId=232545784&issue=neonephos%7Coutreach%7C50):

- Membership to SIGs is open to any member of the Outreach Committee.
- Membership to SIGs is open to any invitee of the SIG.
- The members of a SIG are the ones [listed in GitHub teams](https://github.com/orgs/neonephos/teams/outreach-events).
- Membership is lost through:
  - Two-thirds vote in the SIG or,
  - Two-thirds vote in the Outreach Committee.

The SIGs are free to govern themselves with respect to setting the scope of whom-to-invite. Any such additional requirements [must be listed](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/ambassador_program#how-to-get-involved-in-this-special-interest-group) in the main ```README.md```.

## Voting

As decided in [#50](https://github.com/orgs/neonephos/projects/20/views/1?pane=issue&itemId=232545784&issue=neonephos%7Coutreach%7C50):

- A SIG member has voting rights if the SIG member is member of the Outreach Committee at the same time
- If the SIG consists of less than 3 Outreach Committee members, the SIG may introduce rules to extend the voting to invitees.
- In case of a tie, the voting goes to the Outreach Committee

## Chairperson

- The voting members of the SIG elect a chairperson to reside over the meetings and represent the SIG externally.
  - The chairperson may be any member of the SIG.
- Per default the term coincides with the terms of the Outreach Committee chairperson.
  - Term lengths of less than a year are therefore possible (e.g. for SIGs that are created near the end of the Outreach Committee term).
  - The SIG may introduce additional rules to change this.
- The chairperson may call for new elections anytime.

> ![NOTE]
> The last rule allows for increased flexibility in terms of SIG membership.

## Visibility

- The SIGs are free to decide on whether to make meetings or their recordings public.
- [Publicly accessible meeting minutes must be provided](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/ambassador_program/minutes).

## Closure of SIGs

- SIGs are closed on two-thirds majority vote in the Outreach Committee, or
- The SIGs have no members left.

## Reporting

- SIGs report their progress in the [General Assembly meeting](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/general_assembly/minutes).
