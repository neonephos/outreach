# Marketing Strategy General Condition & Rules

This document describes the general rules and processes governing the NeoNephos Strategy.

> [!NOTE]
> Each NeoNephos Marketing Strategy plan adheres to the rules and processes set out in this document.

## Mission Statement

> The goal of the NeoNephos Marketing Strategy is to create a framework to optimally support the strategic NeoNephos goals set by the NeoNephos Governing Board through the use of marketing tools.

The Marketing Strategy's scope does not include drafting the *overall* strategic vision of NeoNephos.

## Responsibility

The responsibility of creation, modification and maintenance of any NeoNephos Marketing Document is bestowed upon the [Chairperson of the Outreach Committee's Social Media SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/social_media).

> [!Note]
> As there is no Marketing SIG yet, the Social Media SIG is responsible for this document.

## Amendment

This document may be changed by simple majority in the [Social Media SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/social_media) or simple majority in the Outreach Committee.

> [!Note]
> As there is no Marketing SIG yet, the Social Media SIG is responsible for this document.
