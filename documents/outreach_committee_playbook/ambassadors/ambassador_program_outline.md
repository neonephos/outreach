# Ambassador Program Outline

## Application Phase

### From the POV of the Applicant

#### Process

The path to becoming a NeoNephos Foundation Ambassador starts by submitting an official application to the Ambassador SIG. 

More precisely:

- Applications for new ambassadors and renewal of existing ambassadors open February 1st.
- Applications close on March 1st.
- Applications are evaluated throughout March.
 -  This time period may also include ambassador interview and onboarding.
- An ambassador's term "starts" on April 1st and ends on April 1st the following year.

> [!NOTE]
> Ambassador terms are always a maximum of one year but can be renewed by reapplying yearly.

> [!WARNING]
> Applications received outside the application season window are not processed.

#### The 90-day plan

As part of the NNF Ambassador application form, the applicant submits a small 90 day plan, highlighting:

- Activities the applicant hopes to achieve during the first 90 days of the tenure and concrete steps leading towards their fulfilment

#### Ambassador Buddy

Every ambassador is assigned a buddy that supports the ambassador during the tenure. The buddy is the primary point of contact for a new ambassador regarding any questions about the ambassador program or personal goals.

A buddy must fulfil the following characteristics:

- Being member of the Ambassador SIG or,
- Having had more than one year experience being a NNF Ambassador

Buddies are expected to be supportive, available and obey confidentiality, if so requested by the initial ambassador.

### From the POV of the NNF Ambassador SIG

### Who decides on Submissions?

The Ambassador Evaluation Committee votes on applications.

- The members of the Evaluation Committee voted in by the Ambassador SIG.
  - The term of an Evaluation Committee member starts January 1st and ends January 1st the following year.
- Members of the Evaluation Committee must be members of the Ambassador SIG

### Process

1. Prior to the application season in March, the Ambassador SIG aligns on contribution categories where help from Ambassadors is desired.
These categories will then be added to the NNF Ambassador application form.
2. Suitable candidates are invited to a brief interview meeting (~20 min). Throughout March.

## Active Phase

### Day-to-Day Activities of an Ambassador

It is generally desired an Ambassador [participates in at least one of our SIGs or focus areas of work](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics). This way, the ambassador is already integrated into an established structure, which eases management.

### Quarterly Reviews

Quarterly reviews allow the Ambassador SIG to better understand what an individual ambassador is doing and likewise learn of any issues or concerns early.

Every quarter an ambassador is invited by the Ambassador SIG for a brief retroperspective, which includes topics like:

- A quick recap on the realization of the ambassador's set goals.
- Hearing feedback provided by the ambassador.

## Exit Phase

### Exit by Ambassador

An ambassador can voluntarily leave the NNF Ambassador Program by:

- Notifying the Ambassador SIG
- Not renewing ambassadorship at the end of the term

Furthermore:


- The Ambassador SIG conducts an exit interview with the ambassador.
- The Social Media SIG may liaise with the ambassador to create a good-bye blog post, reflecting on the ambassador's achievement and announcing the departure.

### Removal by Committee

The Ambassador SIG can elect to remove an ambassador by a simple majority.

In the rare circumstances, such as a severe code of conduct violation in the eyes of the SIG or LF, an ambassador may be removed immediately without vote.

> [!NOTE]
> Involuntarily removal is a rare occurrence which has not happened yet in the brief history of the NNF Ambassador Program.

#### Clean up activities

- The Ambassador works together with the Ambassador SIG to cleanly exit the program.

This entails:

- Removing the ambassador from the NeoNephos website and related materials
  - This does not necessarily cover:
  - Removing works created by the ambassador (e.g. blog posts)
  - Removing the Ambassador from promotional material related to past events
- Removing the Credly badge issued to the ambassador
- Proper offboarding
