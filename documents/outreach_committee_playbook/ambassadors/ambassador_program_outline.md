# Ambassador Program Outline

## Application Phase

### From the POV of the Applicant

The path to becoming a NeoNephos Ambassador starts by submitting an official application to the Ambassador SIG. 

More precisely:

- Applications for new ambassadors and renewal of existing ambassadors open February 1st.
- Applications close on March 1st.
- Applications are evaluated throughout March.
 -  This time period may also include ambassador interview and onboarding.
- An ambassador's term "starts" on April 1st and ends on April 1st the following year.

> [!NOTE]
> Ambassador terms are always a maximum of one year but can be renewed by reapplying yearly.

> [!WARNING]
> Applications received outside the application season window are not processed.

### From the POV of the NNF Ambassador SIG

### Who decides on Submissions?

The NNF Ambassador SIG internally organizes a subgroup that votes on applications.

### Process

1. Prior to the application season in March, the Ambassador SIG aligns on contribution categories where help from Ambassadors is desired.
These categories will then be added to the NNF Ambassador application form.
2. Suitable candidates are invited to a brief interview meeting (~20 min). Throughout March.
