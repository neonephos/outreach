## How the Outreach Committee proposes events to the Governing Board

The Outreach Committee proposes a prioritized list of events to the Governing Board. 

The Outreach Committee Event SIG is responsible for proposing events to the Governing Board. All proposed events must come from the events board.

### Responsibility

1. Outreach Committee Events SIG is responsible for collecting and prioritizing events of interest to NeoNephos.
2. Outreach Committee Chairperson is responsible for proposing these events to the Governing Board.

### Procedure

1. Outreach Committee Events SIG ensures for each issue:
    1. All form entries from the ```Add Event``` template are filled out to the satisfaction of the Outreach Committee Events SIG.
    2. The engagement type label ```booth``` and / or ```talk``` is given.
    3. [OPTIONAL]: The type label ```community```,```industry```, ```mandatory``` or ```sovereign``` is given.
2. Outreach Committee Chairperson is responsible for creating the events package for consideration of the board:
    1. Go to the [Governing Board Repository page](https://github.com/neonephos/gb/issues).
    2. Fill out the ```Propose Events to the board``` issue template.
    3. Link the events of the [events board](https://github.com/orgs/neonephos/projects/8/views/1) to the newly created issue through ```Add existing issue```.
    4. Ensure that the Governing Board Chairperson is assigned to this request

## Effective managing of event costs

Events are the single most cost-intensive line item of the Outreach committee budget. This is because even a sole event breaks down into many potential, sometimes interlinked cost positions such as:

- Sponsorship level
- Merchandise costs
- Management fees
- Travel costs

Breaking an event up into these positions and making reasonable guesses as to their costs is therefore an important skill. The following sections explain how to best create these guesses and get the best outcome for the lowest price.

### Ask for Discounts

In many cases, the NeoNephos Foundation qualifies for at times steep discounts. Here is a list of historical examples:

|Example| Event GitHub Issue|
|---|---|
|The NeoNephos Foundation received its sponsorship level for the OpenInfraDays for free.| [#18](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/18)|
|Being a LF project, the NeoNephos Foundation received a 50% discount for the KubeCon+CloudNativeCon 'EU 2027 | [#23](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/23)|
|The NeoNephos Foundation was offered to host an Open Source Summit EU 2026 mini-summit for free.|[#2](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/2)|

Therefore, it is always worthwhile **to explicitly asks the event host if the NeoNephos Foundation qualifies for discounts**.

> [!Tip]
> For events hosted by the Linux Foundation, there is a good chance that NNF qualifies for discounts.

### Determine right engagement level

Except for, in NNF's eyes, flagship events such as the [KubeCon+CloudNativeCon](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/23) or select CNCF tier-3 evens such as [KCD Sofia](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/30), there is rarely a need for NNF to sponsor an event.

Instead, focus on these lower cost alternatives:

1. Be a speaker at an event.
2. Use the NNF ambassador network to promote NNF at the event.
3. Promote through our members if they already sponsor an event.
4. Send merchandise for event-provided goodie bags.
5. Use the [Outreach Committee's marketing SIG](https://github.com/marketing-social-media) to explore digital only promotional opportunities.

> [!Tip]
> Each event NNF attends should come with a clear goal. Focus on the minimum event engagement level required in order to fulfill that goal.

### Beware of hidden management fees

For some events such as KubeCon+CloudNativeCon or the Open Source Summit the event registrar offers an optional management service for sponsoring organizations. Such services promise simplified handling of event matters for the sponsor, such as registration, merchandise production or booth setup. Beware of these offers, as they sometimes come with substantial additional cost.

### Shift as many costs to the NNF members as possible

Sometimes, our members attend the same events as NNF does. Use this to NNF's advantage, in order to save on travel costs, tickets and more. Here are some historical examples:

|Example| Event GitHub Issue|
|---|---|
|SAP handled tickets costs for almost all of their NNF booth staff internally instead of drawing on the complimentary free tickets provided through the NNF silver sponsorship level.|[#2](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues/2)|

## Evaluating Success of an Event

Past an event, the feedback loop from the attendants back to the Events SIG must close.

> [!Tip]
> Rule of thumb: If the NNF engagement for an event costs more than €5000 to the foundation, hold a dedicated event retro-perspective meeting. Else, filling out the standard NNF post-event attendance form is enough.
