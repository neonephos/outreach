# Outreach Committee and the Technical Advisory Council

Currently, the Outreach Committee is granted an observer seat in the NeoNephos Technical Advisory Council (TAC).

The duties of that representative include:

- Providing an update on Outreach Committee topics that may be of interest to the TAC
- Encouraging projects to participate in events like submitting a talk or staffing a NeoNephos booth

> [!Note]
> The observer seat granted to an Outreach Committee representative in the TAC is based on an informal understanding. This is not a right granted by the NeoNephos charter. Therefore, this role is not formalized.
