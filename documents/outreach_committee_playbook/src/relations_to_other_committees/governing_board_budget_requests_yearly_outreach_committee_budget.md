# Yearly Outreach Committee Budget

The yearly Outreach Committee budget is the most important budget to request from the Outreach Committee to the Governing Board. It bundles all funding requirements by all SIGs of the Outreach Committee (and the committee itself) for an entire year into a single request to the Governing Board.

## Statements by the Linux Foundation on the yearly budget

The NeoNephos LF PMO John Mertic has stated that unused budget between categories in the Yearly Outreach Committee Budget carries over.

> Concrete example: If an event requested €1000 but ultimately only cost €300, the remaining €700 can be used for other items in the Outreach Committee Yearly Budget.>

### Who oversees spent

The NeoNephos LF PMO John Mertic has stated that the NeoNephos LF PMO keeps track of spending within the Outreach Committee Budget.

### How to submit 

The Yearly Outreach Committee Budget is submitted via an issue in the [Governing Board repository (private link)](https://github.com/neonephos/gb) by the Outreach Committee Chairperson or delegate thereof.

### Due date

The yearly budget for the coming year should be proposed between October to November of the preceding year.

>[!Warning]
> Failure to request the Yearly Outreach Committee Budget in time could lead to delays or even impossibility of the GB granting the funds. Reasoning: The GB itself must align on a yearly budget for their committee (usually by December of the preceding year) too. The Yearly Outreach Committee Budget is usually a part of that. 

## Must-have-categories for the yearly budget

The following categories are commonly considered in the yearly budget.

|Category|Requestor|Usual Line Items|
|---|---|--|
|Events|Events SIG| [Event sponsorships](../events/events.html#how-the-outreach-committee-proposes-events-to-the-governing-board) and merchandise|
|Ambassador|Ambassador SIG | Ambassador merchandise and [mini-stipend](./../ambassador/ambassador_funding_guide.html)|
|Merchandise|All SIGs|General Merchandise|
|General|Outreach Committee|Miscellanous, e.g. budget for press releases|

Note: In the Yearly Outreach Committee Budget the requests in the table above are bundled into a single request.

> [!Tip]
> The Outreach Committee chairperson should work together with the SIGs to align on the items.

## How much is reasonable to request?

It must be kept in mind that the NeoNephos Foundation exclusively generates revenue from member fees.

- For comparison: The 2027 Outreach Committee Budget was around €120.000 (incl. an additional request for KubeCon+CloudNativeCon 2027).

> [!Tip]
> Use [the current NNF members](https://neonephos.org/members) and associated [pricing table](https://neonephos.org/join) to get an estimate on how much the foundation collects in a given year.

> [!Tip]
> As a rule of thumb: The Outreach Committee yearly budget should not exceed 25% of the revenue obtained from membership fees.

> [!Note]
> In 2026 the NeoNephos Foundation generated around €500k from membership fees.
