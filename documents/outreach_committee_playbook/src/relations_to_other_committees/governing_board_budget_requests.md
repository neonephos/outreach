# Budget Requests

The Outreach Committee has no independent funding mechanism. *All* funding must be requested from and approved by the Governing Board **prior to the expense occuring**. This guide lists common funding topics and explains how to request these items from the Governing Board. 

## Responsibility

The Outreach Committee chairperson is responsible for organizing the funding requests and proposing them to the Governing Board.

### Common Funding Requests

- [Yearly Outreach Committee Budget](./governing_board_budget_requests_yearly_outreach_committee_budget.html)

### Unit of Account

The requests are to be made with in Euro (€). If the costs for some line items are in another currency (e.g. costs of LF events are often in United States Dollar), a conversion to Euro should be made using the exchange rate at the day of proposal.

### Useful Information

- Approving the budget in the Governing Board may take a long time. For example, the 2027 Outreach Committee budget was submitted in October 2026 and approved in December 2026.
  - Therefore, always be very early with requests!
