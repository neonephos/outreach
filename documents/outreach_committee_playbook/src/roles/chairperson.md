# The Outreach Committee Chairperson

The Outreach Committee chairperson as the organizatorial lead and primary point of contact of the Outreach Committee.

## Duties of the Chairperson

Amongst other things, the chairperson is responsible for:

- [Proposing the Yearly Outreach Committee Budget to the Governing Board](./../relations_to_other_committees/governing_board_budget_requests_yearly_outreach_committee_budget.html)
- Proposing the Outreach Committee roadmap
- Holding the monthly Outreach Committee General Assembly meeting
- Representing the Outreach Committee in the Governing Board
- Being the primary point of contact of the Outreach Committee

> If the Outreach Committee chair is not part of a SIG, then above lists covers most of the required activities.

## Existence of Role

According to the [NeoNephos Charter](https://cdn.platform.linuxfoundation.org/agreements/neonephos-foundation.pdf) the Outreach Committee chairperson role and election procedure thereof is left for Governing Board to determine. Historically, the Governing Board has delegated the election of the chairperson to the Outreach Committee.

## Expected Workload

This table list a realistic expetced workload in hours of the Outreach Committee chairperson:

|Task|Expected Workload per week \[hours\] |
|---|---|
|Attending Governining Board meetings|1|
|Asynchronous discussion with Governing Board | 2|
|Asynchronous discussing with Outreach Committee | 3 |
|Preparing slidedecks (e.g. Outreach Committee General Assembly)| 1|
|Working on SIG topics| 3|

Above is an estimate, actual mileage may vary widely. Nevertheless, it is a reasonable expectation that being a Outreach Committee chairperson consumes about a full working day per week.
