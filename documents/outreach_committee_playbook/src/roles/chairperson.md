# The Outreach Committee Chairperson

The Outreach Committee chairperson is the organizatorial lead and primary point of contact of the Outreach Committee. The current chairperson is listed [on the NeoNephos website](https://neonephos.org/outreach_committee).

## Duties of the Chairperson

Amongst other things, the chairperson is responsible for:

- [Proposing the Yearly Outreach Committee Budget to the Governing Board](./../relations_to_other_committees/governing_board_budget_requests_yearly_outreach_committee_budget.html)
- Proposing the Outreach Committee roadmap
- Holding the monthly Outreach Committee General Assembly meeting
- Representing the Outreach Committee in the Governing Board
- Being the primary point of contact of the Outreach Committee

> If the Outreach Committee chair is not part of a SIG, then above list covers most of the required activities.

## Existence of Role

According to the [NeoNephos Charter](https://cdn.platform.linuxfoundation.org/agreements/neonephos-foundation.pdf) the Outreach Committee chairperson role and election procedure is left for Governing Board to determine. Within the context of NeoNepohs, the Governing Board has delegated the nomination and election of the chairperson to the Outreach Committee.

## Expected Workload

This table lista an estimate on the workload in hours of the Outreach Committee chairperson:

|Task|Expected Workload per week \[hours\] |
|---|---|
|Attending Governining Board meetings|1|
|Asynchronous discussion with Governing Board | 2|
|Asynchronous discussing with Outreach Committee | 3 |
|Preparing slidedecks (e.g. Outreach Committee General Assembly)| 1|
|Working on SIG topics| 3|

Actual mileage may vary widely. Nevertheless, it is a reasonable expectation that being a Outreach Committee chairperson consumes about a full working day per week.
