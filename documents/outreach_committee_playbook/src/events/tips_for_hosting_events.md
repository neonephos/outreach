## Virtual Events

Virtual events are the primary type of events hosted by the NeoNephos Foundation. They are a flexible instrument that can be used to promote and introduce topics, at times on short notice.

> [!Tip]
> Because of their little draw on foundational resources and staff, virtual events should be preferred whenever possible.

### Advantages of virtual events

- low cost
- easily accessible worldwide
- low preparation time (usually weeks at most)

### Disadvantages of virtual events

- post event networking much harder
- low barrier to attend means many participants might not be truly interested in the topic
- less impactful media / videos (no speaker on a grand stage - just a person behind a screen)
- more susceptible to technical glitches like unstable networks

### Rules to host virtual events

- Events under the NNF name must be approved by either the Governing Board, Outreach Committee or Technical Advisory Council. The scope of the event determines which committee is responsible for approval.
- All virtual events must be hosted [through https://app.lfx.dev/](https://app.lfx.dev/).
  - Ideally, event recording is turned on.
- The event must appear on [https://neoenphos.org/events](https://neoenphos.org/events). To do this create a PR [here](https://github.com/neonephos/neonephos_website/tree/main/src/docs/events).
- If the event is not already approved by NNF, a formal request must be made here [here](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues).

> [!Tip]
> Only group managers have the privileges to schedule events through [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev/). If you need an event scheduled but do not possess these rights, contact [support@neonephos.org](mailto: support@neonephos.org).

> [!Warning]
> Do not host events through Microsoft Teams, Google hangouts or similar non LFX tools.

> [!Note]
> The foundation is aware that the [NNF MCK Meeting](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_11_17_MCK_NeoNephos_Series) does not use [app.lfx.dev](https://app.lfx.dev) at this point in time. This is an exception.


## On-Site Events

The NeoNephos Foundation is also open to host on-site events. Due to their much higher requirements in preparation, logistics and execution, however, these only make up a small fraction of events hosted by NNF.

### Examples

There have been no on-site events hosted by NNF thus far. For 2027 planning for a NNF hackathon and NNF supported OSSummit Mini Summit is conducted.


### Assisting Events of NNF Projects

The Outreach Committee is not responsible for events hosted by its projects, such as project specific community calls. Nevertheless, it may support events hosted by its projects or friends of the foundation.
