# Areas of Work

This chapter lists areas of work of the Outreach Committee. Structurally, these may be organized into:

- [Special Interest Groups (SIGs)](https://github.com/neonephos/metadata)
- [Working Groups (WGs)](https://github.com/neonephos/metadata)
- [Other thematically releant topics](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/topics)

This chapter is more concerned with the concrete tasks than the working structure behind them.
