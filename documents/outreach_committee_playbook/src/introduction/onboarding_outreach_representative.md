# POV: You are a new Outreach Rep.

Are you are a new Outreach Committee member (or nominated by your company to become one)? Don't feel lost, as you have come to the right place!

> [!Tip]
> To fully understand the workings of the Outreach Committee it is recommended to work through this book.

## What to expect as an Outreach Committee Representative

Outreach Committee representatives are given the opportunity to:

- Join the monthly Outreach Committee General Assembly meeting
- Become a member of the NeoNephos GitHub organization
- Access to the Outreach Committee exclusive Zulip channel and mailing list
- Access to summary information on what happens in the NeoNephos Governing Board and other Commitees
- Ability to the strategy of NeoNephos Outreach activities through participation in the SIGs and exclusive working groups

> [!Hint]
> Attendance of any Outreach Committee meetings is voluntary.

## Eligibility for Outreach Committee Representatives

According to [4a.i of the NeoNephos Charter](https://cdn.platform.linuxfoundation.org/agreements/neonephos-foundation.pdf?__hstc=81619592.11739e1f4ca03192f4e918409f725053.1748961872693.1755169332458.1755604645613.29&__hssc=81619592.2.1755604645613&__hsfp=1879682750):

> i) The Outreach Committee will include one appointed voting representative from each
> Premier Sponsor. Each General Sponsor may appoint a non-voting representative to
> observe and contribute ideas to the Outreach Committee.

Therefore, if your company is either a Premier or General member of the NeoNephos Foundation it is eligible to name a representative for the Outreach Committee.

## How to report your named representative to the foundation

In order to formally appoint a member of a qualifying member organization to the NNF Outreach Committee, relay your chosen representative to either:

- The NNF Linux Foundation PMO.
- The Outreach Committee chairperson.

### What you need to submit

- Name of the representative
- The profile on [https://app.lfx.dev/](https://app.lfx.dev/)

> [!Hint]
> Add a profile picture to the representatives account on [https://app.lfx.dev/](https://app.lfx.dev/) in order to have that image displayed [on the NeoNephos website](https://neonephos.org/outreach_committee).

## How to change the Outreach Committee Representative

To change the Outreach Committee representative of a member organization, relay your new representative to either:

- The NNF Linux Foundation PMO
- The Outreach Committee chairperson

Please include information on when the term of the new representative should start.

## How to verify that you are an Outreach Committee member

Rule of thumb: You are formally an Outreach Committee member when your name is mentioned [in this LF API call](https://api-gw.platform.linuxfoundation.org/project-service/v2/public/projects/lfGnr1MCq2lbKUf6f7/committees/457217c1-2421-4685-958d-a1ca8cbe691e/members). If you see your name inside this returned API Call: Congratulations, you are officially a member of the NNF Outreach Committee!

> [!Hint]
> After your Outreach Committee Representative is [stored internally](https://api-gw.platform.linuxfoundation.org/project-service/v2/public/projects/lfGnr1MCq2lbKUf6f7/committees/457217c1-2421-4685-958d-a1ca8cbe691e/members), the representative will also be displayed [on the NeoNephos website](https://neonephos.org/outreach_committee). This process happens automatically. If your representative is still not shown on the NeoNephos website after a few days, open a pull request [here](https://github.com/neonephos/neonephos_website/blob/main/src/docs/outreach_committee.md).
