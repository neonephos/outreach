# History and Purpose

The Outreach Committee was established in April 2026, almost on the day of the [one-year anniversary of the NeoNephos Foundation](https://linuxfoundation.eu/newsroom/the-linux-foundation-announces-the-launch-of-neonephos-to-advance-digital-autonomy-in-europe). It is defined in [4a.ii of the NeoNephos Charter](https://cdn.platform.linuxfoundation.org/agreements/neonephos-foundation.pdf?__hstc=81619592.11739e1f4ca03192f4e918409f725053.1748961872693.1755169332458.1755604645613.29&__hssc=81619592.2.1755604645613&__hsfp=1879682750), which conveniently defines its scope as:

> The Outreach Committee will be responsible for the design, development, and 
> execution of community outreach efforts on behalf of the Governing Board. The 
> Outreach Committee is expected to coordinate closely with the Governing Board and 
> technical communities to maximize the outreach and visibility of the Technical 
> Project throughout the ecosystem.

In practical terms, the Outreach Committee is responsible for:

- [Hosting and managing of events](./../events/events.md)
- The [NeoNephos Ambassador Program](./../ambassador/ambassador_program_outline.md)
- Everything [related to social media](./../social_media/social_media.md)
- Marketing
- Establishing a strategy for collecting data for [internal consumption](./../kpi/kpi.md)

It is important to note that **Projects are still responsible for their own marketing and outreach efforts**. But they may coordinate with the Outreach Committee to amplify their message through foundation-wide outlet channels.
