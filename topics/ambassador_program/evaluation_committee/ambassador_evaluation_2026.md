# Ambassador Evaluation 2026

This document records the ambassador evaluation process for the year 2026.

## Checklist

- [ ] Infer "need for ambassador help" from the NNF SIGs
- [ ] Review ambassador applications
- [ ] Create 90-day action-plan with ambassador applicants
- [ ] Vote on ambassadors

## General Information

The submission for NNF Ambassador applications in 2026 is closed. The goal is to process the current pipeline of pending applications from the spring - summer 2026 period.

## Desired Attributes

This lists the attributes an ideal ambassador should have according to the respective SIGs.

### Marketing & Social Media SIG

The [Marketing & Social Media SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/social_media) would like to welcome **1** ambassador with at least one of the following attributes:

- Wants to contribute to [blog posts](https://neonephos.org/blog) with a technical or community focus.
- Create video recordings of members.
  - This might require some travel which could be [covered by NNF](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/blob/main/documents/outreach_committee_playbook/ambassadors/ambassador_funding_guide.md).
- Conduct marketing activities such as:
  - Creating a marketing plan.
  - Align with other foundations and projects for cross promotional efforts.
- Conduct inter foundation efforts, such as:
  - Align with NNF projects on news to post

### Events SIG

The [Events SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/events) would like to welcome **2** ambassadors with at least one of the following attributes each:

- Research events of interest to NNF
- Host smaller NNF events, hackathons and community gatherings
- Help in the organization of [events from this list](https://github.com/neonephos/events/issues).

### KPI SIG

The [KPI SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/kpi) would like to welcome **1** ambassador with at least one of the following attributes:

- Likes to support to build up the NNF KPI framework.
