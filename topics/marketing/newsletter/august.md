*Newsletter Design Guidelines*

*Please follow the guidelines below when designing the newsletter:*

*Typography*

1. *Font: Please use Aptos wherever possible. If Aptos is not available or cannot be embedded, use Arial as the fallback font.*
2. *Body text: 12 px*
3. *Main heading (H1): 16 px*
4. *Subheading (H2): 14 px*
5. *Font style: Do not use italic text. Please use regular or bold text only.*

*Please keep the typography consistent throughout the newsletter and use the specified font sizes for headings and body copy.*

*Header and Footer*

*The header and footer will be provided as separate files. Please integrate them into the newsletter design where technically possible.*
*Please add an interactive LinkedIn Icon to the Icon List in the bottom*

*Ensure that:*

1. *The header is positioned consistently at the top of the newsletter.*
2. *The footer is positioned consistently at the bottom.*
3. *The original proportions and visual appearance*

# NeoNephos Foundation Newsletter

Welcome to our newest issue of the NNF Newsletter 📰! As we are in the closing months of an eventful summer, let's take a look back on what happened in the month of **August**.

## Our August NeoNephos Community Call 

featured updates from across the foundation, including the introduction of SVA as a new General Member, updates on the Project Lifecycle Policy, upcoming events, and the new Project Community Managers Monthly Sync. The call also included a project spotlight on Open Control Plane, presented by Max Techritz, followed by an open discussion on long-term support for open-source projects and products.
🎥 Watch the recording: [[Watch on YouTube](https://youtu.be/JdLJ-6aZw90)]

## KPI SIG Formed

because thoughtful decisions are often based on data. The [KPI SIG](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/kpi) ensures that the various data sources emerging from the works of the Outreach Committee SIGs are harvested, stored and their potential exploited. For more details on how you can be part of this SIG see [here](https://github.com/neonephos/outreach/tree/main/topics/kpi).

## Event Season is coming up

and we've started with the preparations! 

We'll be attending the following events:

- **BWI Industry Days** in Berlin on September 01-02;
- **Container Days** in Hamburg on September 02-04;
- **Kubernetes Community Days** in Sofia on September 29;
- **Open Source Summit** in Prague on October 07;
- **OpenInfra Days** in Saarbrücken on October 22-25;
- and **Dutch Cloud Native Day** in Utrecht on October 29-30.

We'll share more information with you on [our website](https://neonephos.org/events) as the events are coming closer. 
We will be represented in different forms at the events – but we are always happy to meet you!
So no matter if you just want to say hi, if you're thinking about donating your project, or if you're interested in becoming part of this organization – don't be shy!
