*Newsletter Design Guidelines*

*Please follow the guidelines below when designing the newsletter:*

*Typography*

1. *Font: Please use Aptos wherever possible. If Aptos is not available or cannot be embedded, use Arial as the fallback font.*
2. *Body text: 12 px*
3. *Main heading (H1): 16 px*
4. *Subheading (H2): 14 px*
5. *Font style: Do not use italic text. Please use regular or bold text only.*

*Please keep the typography consistent throughout the newsletter and use the specified font sizes for headings and body copy.*

*Header and Footer*

*The header and footer will be provided as separate files. Please integrate them into the newsletter design where technically possible.*
*Please add an interactive LinkedIn Icon to the Icon List in the bottom.*

*Ensure that:*

1. *The header is positioned consistently at the top of the newsletter.*
2. *The footer is positioned consistently at the bottom.*
3. *The original proportions and visual appearance*

# September and October Recap

Welcome back! This time our newsletter is jam-packed with news, so without further ado:

## NeoNephos Foundation at KCD Sofia

[KCD Sofia was a blast!](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_09_17_KCD_Sofia) And we were there. *Thank you* to the organizers, who successfully managed to set up this packed event and to everybody who visited our booth and had a chat, it was awesome! It always feels great to introduce the relevance of cloud native sovereignty to an eager audience. See you until next year?!

## NeoNephos at the Open Source Summit in Prague

The NeoNephos Foundation was present at the [Open Source Summit in Prague](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_10_07_Open_Source_Summit_Europe). Connecting with our peers always gets our spirits up and this time was no different! The enthusiasm was especially apparent at our NeoNephos booth, where we met many new and old familiar faces. Thank you, Prague!

## Recordings of Meetings of the Technical Advisory Council now available

To increase transparency about what is happening within our committees, recordings of our monthly Technical Advisory Meetings are [now publicly available](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCqTJlPfPRAynwpcNI0O7xkw). These one hours sessions between representatives of our members and projects are important to shape the technical vision of NNF. By making recordings of these meetings public, you now get an even more deeper insight about what is actually happening in our foundation.

## World of Open Source Survey

How clearly has Europe acknowledged the imperative of digital sovereignty? As it turns out, very cristal clear: That is why the NeoNephos Foundation is one of the sponsors of the Open Source in Europe 2026 report. Thanks to this survey, we can see that Neonephos' vision *to make cloud native sovereign* is shared by many of our industry peers. Read the full report [here](https://lnkd.in/gPx6BV8z?trk=public_post_reshare-text).

## Recording of "Butter bei die Fische Meeting" now available

Born out of an idea from the Container Days in Hamburg, the recording of the first iteration of the [Butter bei die Fische Meeting](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_09_23_Butter_bei_die_Fische_Meeting) series [is available now](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCqTJlPfPRAynwpcNI0O7xkw). This open-to-anybody-event features important discussions between NNF representatives and the community. See more details [here](https://neonephos.org/events/2026_09_23_Butter_bei_die_Fische_Meeting).
